STAGE 3: BATCH IMAGE PROMPT GENERATION (CONCISE 50-90 WORDS)

Generate concise, high-density, action-first image generation prompts for the supplied batch of planned storyboard scenes.

PROMPT CONSTRUCTION GUIDELINES:
1. TARGET LENGTH (50-90 WORDS):
   - Every prompt must be approximately 50 to 90 words of pure visual instruction.
   - ZERO FILLER: Strictly eliminate repetitive fluff about sepia tones, silver gelatin grain, scratches, foxing, or chemical stains.
2. ACTION & SUBJECT FIRST:
   - Describe what is physically visible and actively happening in frame.
   - Ground characters in the Character Bible (exact faces, beards, and attire).
   - Ground environments in the Location Bible (stone, timber, brick, terrain).
3. STYLISTIC EXECUTION & SETTING ACCURACY (MANDATORY):
   - STRICTLY HONOR THE CHOSEN VISUAL STYLE:
     * When the selected style is "Century-Old Photograph" / "Vintage Photograph": Render as an authentic century-old archival photograph or antique plate print. Timeless warm sepia, aged albumen, or rich silver-toned monochrome palette with deep dynamic range. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: NO modern vibrant RGB saturated colors (no bright modern blue oceans, no vivid digital greens, no 2024 movie color grading).
     * When another style is selected (e.g. Cinematic Photorealism, 2D Vector): Execute in that designated medium.
   - CLEAN ARCHIVAL FINISH (NO FAKE DAMAGE):
     * Crisp, sharp focal clarity on the primary subject, intact border framing, authentic photographic texture.
     * STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: NO artificial chemical spots, NO dirty splatters, NO fake scratches across faces, and NO torn paper edges. Keep the image dignified, clean, atmospheric, and high-resolution.
   - SETTING-AUTHENTIC DETAILS:
     * Setting accuracy in architecture, tools, garments, and materials (e.g. brass Fresnel lenses, oiled-wool coats, iron spiral staircases for coastal watchtower; or bronze armor and classical stone pillars for antiquity).
     * STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: NO anachronistic items, modern garments, or equipment that conflict with the established setting period.
4. DETAIL CHECKLIST (woven concisely):
   1) Main subject (Character Bible reference).
   2) Specific physical action / event underway.
   3) Environment & architectural textures (Location Bible reference).
   4) Composition & camera angle.
   5) Lighting direction & color palette.
   6) Setting authenticity & clean rendering (clean, sharp, high resolution).

OUTPUT SCHEMA:
Return a strictly valid JSON object with the following structure:
{
  "batch_index": <int>,
  "prompts": [
    {
      "scene_id": "001",
      "visual_subject": "...",
      "prompt": "Concise 50-90 word visual prompt describing subject, physical action, environment, composition, lighting, period accuracy, and clean rendering..."
    }
  ]
}
