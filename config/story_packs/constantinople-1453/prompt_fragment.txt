Chronology: 1. Ottoman artillery preparations & camp outside walls -> 2. Siege begins with bombardment -> 3. Byzantine defenders frantically repair breached masonry -> 4. NO premature reveals of overland ship transport or discarded crowns.
Attacking forces (Ottoman forces, Sultan Mehmed II) = RED stickmen; Defending forces (Byzantine defenders, Emperor Constantine XI) = BLUE stickmen.
