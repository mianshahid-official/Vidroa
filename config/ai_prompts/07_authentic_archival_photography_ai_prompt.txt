Authentic Archival Photography has NO torn edges and NO paper borders whatsoever. The image IS the archival photograph itself, filling the full 16:9 widescreen frame directly from optical edge to optical edge.

================================================================================
STYLE DIRECTIVES: AUTHENTIC ARCHIVAL PHOTOGRAPHY
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- Authentic period photograph: Silver gelatin print, albumen print, or wet-plate collodion emulsion.
- Full-Frame Composition: The photographic plate fills the frame completely from edge to edge (no margins, no frames, no backing cards).
- Tonal Palette: Warm sepia, aged silver tones, soft albumen amber, or rich vintage monochrome.
- Physical Imperfections: Realistic optical film grain, genuine chemical dust spots, subtle emulsion pinholes, delicate vertical scratches, and corner lens vignetting.
- Lighting: Natural period lighting, authentic glass lens depth of field, gentle contrast rolloff in highlights and shadows.

2. SUBJECT MATTER & PERIOD FIDELITY:
- Real historical moments, figures, battlefields, expeditions, inventions, city streets, and ceremonies.
- Captured strictly as a contemporary period camera would have captured them (e.g. wet-plate tripod camera in the 1860s, large-format bellows camera in the 1900s, Leica rangefinder in the 1930s).
- Historically accurate period garments, equipment, hairstyles, and surroundings.

3. STILLS TREATMENT:
- Stills are held completely still at all times, with zero pan, zoom, or simulated Ken Burns drift.
- Dynamic motion is provided through real B-roll video footage via the `VIDEO of ... KEYWORDS ...` rule.

4. STRICT NEVER-DO LIST (NEGATIVE RULES):
- NEVER include torn paper edges, chipped borders, frayed paper borders, or deckle edges (torn edges belong exclusively to century-old photograph style).
- NEVER include paper borders, frame matting, or photo card margins — the photo fills the frame.
- NEVER include illustrations, woodcuts, engravings, or drawings.
- NEVER include modern saturated RGB color grading (no modern digital colors).
- NEVER include modern cameras, modern gadgets, modern wristwatches, or modern clothing.
- NEVER include 3D CGI rendering, cartoon elements, or anime styles.
